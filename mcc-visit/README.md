## The one-line positioning (say this first)

> "Anukriti is a **safety engine for fluoropyrimidines**. It takes a patient's
> DPYD test result and answers: *can this patient clear 5-FU/capecitabine
> normally, or should the dose be reduced/avoided?* We do **not** predict
> whether the tumour responds — that's a different question."

## The core wedge (why MCC specifically)

MCC may already hold **~400–500 DPYD-tested patients** with **toxicity
follow-up**, tested on a **7-variant real-time PCR panel**. The highest-value
question we can answer together:

> "How many of your patients had **Grade 3+ toxicity while testing negative
> on all 7 panel variants**?"

A non-zero answer = missed-risk patients = the case for (a) a wider /
South-Asian-aware variant panel and (b) a joint Indian-cohort publication.
Anukriti turns their **existing PCR dataset** into a genotype→toxicity map —
no NGS required to start.

## Hard honesty rules for the visit (do not break)

1. We are **not** a clinical decision-support system; outputs are research
   artifacts. No dosing is prescribed by the tool.
2. We do **not** overclaim non-core variants (*9A, *4, *5, *6, M166V). They
   are labelled "research evidence / outcome-correlation needed" until MCC's
   outcomes support upgrading them. This makes MCC the **evidence partner**,
   not a validator of a black box.
3. **No PHI** ever leaves MCC — anonymized IDs only, summaries not raw
   sequences.
4. Every claim cites a rule; every rule cites a paper; every refusal is named.
