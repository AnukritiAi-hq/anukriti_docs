Title:   PharmVar Tutorial on CYP2D6 Structural Variation Testing and
         Recommendations on Reporting
Authors: Amy J. Turner, Charity Nofziger, Bryn E. Ramey, Reynold C. Ly,
         Chad A. Bousman, José A.G. Agúndez, ... Andrea Gaedigk (PharmVar
         CYP2D6 Structural Variation Working Group)
Journal: Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics, 114(6):1220-1237 (Dec 2023)
DOI:     10.1002/cpt.3044
PMID:    37669183
PMCID:   PMC10840842

Summary:
  The authoritative PharmVar consensus on how CYP2D6 structural variation
  (whole-gene deletions *5, duplications/multiplications *1xN/*2xN,
  CYP2D6-CYP2D7 hybrids and tandem arrangements) should be TESTED and,
  critically, REPORTED. Defines the canonical nomenclature and the
  expected diplotype-reporting format for SV-containing genotypes, and
  reviews the assay/bioinformatic methods capable of resolving them.

Relevance to Anukriti (Rung 2):
  This is the REPORTING / INTERPRETATION STANDARD for CYP2D6 SVs — the
  CYP2D6 analogue of the CPIC tables we pin for DPYD/warfarin. Whatever
  caller we adopt (Cyrius, StellarPGx, Aldy, or StarPhase), its SV output
  must be normalised to the nomenclature and diplotype-reporting rules
  defined here before it enters the deterministic engine. Pin this as the
  provenance source for the CYP2D6 SV -> star-allele -> phenotype mapping,
  the same way DPYD_CLINICAL_ACTIONS cites the CPIC fluoropyrimidine
  guideline.
