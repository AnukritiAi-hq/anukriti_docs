## 📚 Papers

### 1. Moorjani-2013-AJHG-Genetic-Evidence-for-Recent-Population-Mixture-in-India.pdf

- **Title:** Genetic Evidence for Recent Population Mixture in India
- **Authors:** Priya Moorjani, Kumarasamy Thangaraj, Nick Patterson, Mark Lipson, Po-Ru Loh, Periyasamy Govindaraj, Bonnie Berger, David Reich, Lalji Singh
- **Journal:** The American Journal of Human Genetics, 93, 422–438 (Sept 5, 2013)
- **DOI:** [10.1016/j.ajhg.2013.07.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2013.07.006)
- **Summary:** Genome-wide data from 73 Indian groups; estimates ANI–ASI mixture dates of ~1,900–4,200 years ago. Foundational paper for understanding Indian population structure (ANI/ASI ancestry).
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** Core reference for Indian population genetics — directly informs population-aware matching and the ANI/ASI ancestry deconvolution that Anukriti's pipeline depends on.

---

### 2. Martin-2017-AJHG-Human-Demographic-History-Impacts-Genetic-Risk-Prediction.pdf

- **Title:** Human Demographic History Impacts Genetic Risk Prediction across Diverse Populations
- **Authors:** Alicia R. Martin, Christopher R. Gignoux, Raymond K. Walters, Genevieve L. Wojcik, Benjamin M. Neale, Simon Gravel, Mark J. Daly, Carlos D. Bustamante, **Eimear E. Kenny** (corresponding)
- **Journal:** The American Journal of Human Genetics, 100, 635–649 (Apr 6, 2017)
- **DOI:** [10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.03.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.03.004)
- **Summary:** Demonstrates that polygenic risk scores derived from European GWASs do not transfer well to other populations — biases are unpredictable and can go in either direction. Calls for diverse cohorts and generalized risk-prediction methods.
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** Direct evidence/equity case for population-aware genomics; co-authored by Eimear Kenny (lead of the Pangenome Consortium and Mount Sinai Institute for Genomic Health).

### 3. Martin-2019-NatGenet-Clinical-Use-of-Current-Polygenic-Risk-Scores-Citation.txt

- **Title:** Clinical use of current polygenic risk scores may exacerbate health disparities
- **Authors:** Alicia R. Martin, Masahiro Kanai, Yoichiro Kamatani, Yukinori Okada, Benjamin M. Neale, Mark J. Daly
- **Journal:** Nature Genetics, 51(4), 584–591 (April 2019; Epub Mar 29, 2019)
- **DOI:** [10.1038/s41588-019-0379-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0379-x)
- **PMID:** [30926966](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30926966/)
- **PMCID:** [PMC6563838](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6563838/)
- **Summary:** Sister paper to Martin 2017 — sharper, more clinical-impact-oriented. Quantifies how PRS performance gaps across ancestries translate into measurable health-disparity amplification when current scores are deployed clinically without ancestry-aware calibration. Erratum: *Nat Genet* 53(5):763 (May 2021).
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** **Clinical-equity citation.** Where Martin 2017 establishes the population-genetics mechanism, Martin 2019 establishes the clinical-deployment harm. Together they are the two-paper case for why population-aware infrastructure is not optional. Cite both alongside Kerdoncuff 2025 in any deployment-risk argument.
- **Storage note:** PDF not in the repo (NIH PMC paywall + size); this entry stores only the NCBI citation export. Pull the PDF locally from PMC6563838 when needed.

### 4. Poplin-2018-NatBiotech-DeepVariant-Universal-SNP-Indel-Caller-DNN.pdf

- **Title:** A universal SNP and small-indel variant caller using deep neural networks (DeepVariant)
- **Authors:** Ryan Poplin, Pi-Chuan Chang, David Alexander, Scott Schwartz, Thomas Colthurst, Alexander Ku, Dan Newburger, Jojo Dijamco, Nam Nguyen, Pegah T. Afshar, Sam S. Gross, Lizzie Dorfman, Cory Y. McLean, Mark A. DePristo (Verily / Google)
- **Journal:** Nature Biotechnology (Sept 24, 2018)
- **DOI:** [10.1038/nbt.4235](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.4235)
- **Summary:** Introduces DeepVariant — a CNN (Inception architecture) that calls SNPs/indels from read pileup images. Outperforms GATK best-practices and generalizes across genome builds, technologies, and species.
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** Reference variant-caller for the genomics pipeline; benchmark for accuracy and generalizability when handling diverse sequencing data.

### 5. Narasimhan-2019-Science-Formation-of-Human-Populations-in-South-and-Central-Asia.pdf

- **Title:** The Formation of Human Populations in South and Central Asia
- **Authors:** Vagheesh M. Narasimhan, Nick Patterson, Priya Moorjani, et al. (David Reich lab + 100+ collaborators)
- **Journal:** Science, 365(6457), Sept 6, 2019
- **DOI:** [10.1126/science.aat7487](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat7487)
- **Summary:** Sequenced 523 ancient humans. Shows primary ancestry source for South Asians is a prehistoric gradient between Iranian hunter-gatherers and Southeast Asians, later mixed with Steppe pastoralist descendants (~4000 years ago). Parallels Bronze Age Eastern Europe.
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** Deepens the ancient-DNA backbone for ANI/ASI ancestry; useful for Anukriti's population-stratification reference panels.

---

### 6. Kerdoncuff-2025-Cell-50000-Years-Evolutionary-History-of-India-Health-Disease.pdf

- **Title:** 50,000 years of evolutionary history of India: Impact on health and disease variation
- **Authors:** Elise Kerdoncuff, Laurits Skov, Nick Patterson, Priya Moorjani (corresponding), Andrea Ganna, et al. (large pan-India clinical consortium incl. AIIMS, NIMHANS, BHU, etc.)
- **Journal:** Cell, 188(13), 3389–3404.e6 (June 26, 2025)
- **DOI:** [10.1016/j.cell.2025.04.027](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.04.027)
- **Summary:** Most recent and most comprehensive evolutionary-genomics study of India; directly links 50,000 years of Indian demographic history to present-day health and disease variation. Built with all-India clinical partners.
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** **Cornerstone reference.** Combines population genetics + clinical phenotypes specifically for Indian populations — the exact intersection Anukriti operates in. The Indian clinical-site network here mirrors Anukriti's CRO-side ambitions.

### 7. Zack-2026-npjDigitalMedicine-Agentic-AI-System-for-Automated-Pharmacogenomic-Recommendation.pdf

- **Title:** An agentic AI system for automated pharmacogenomic recommendation generation
- **Authors:** M. Zack, A. Savinkov, D. Stupichev, et al.
- **Journal:** npj Digital Medicine (2026, Article in Press)
- **DOI:** [10.1038/s41746-026-02590-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02590-w)
- **Summary:** Describes an agentic AI system that automates pharmacogenomic recommendation generation — combining LLM-based agents with structured genomic/drug knowledge.
- **Relevance to Anukriti:** **Direct architectural reference.** Most aligned paper to Anukriti's own agentic-AI + genomics pipeline. Useful for benchmarking design choices, evaluation strategy, and positioning vs. existing agentic pharmacogenomics systems.
