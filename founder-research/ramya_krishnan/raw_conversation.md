## Question 2 — Reliability of VCF Files & Sequencing Platforms

### Atul Alexander
"When a patient's DNA is sequenced and we get a VCF file, does the sequencing method affect how reliable the VCF output is? Which methods would you trust enough to make clinical decisions from?"

### Ramya Krishnan
"Yes, I do think the sequencing method significantly affects how reliable a VCF output is.

Different sequencing platforms have different error profiles:

- Illumina → short reads with high base accuracy; strong for SNP detection
- Nanopore → long reads useful for repetitive and structural regions, but with higher raw error rates
- PacBio → combines long reads with good accuracy, though it is more expensive

Because of these differences, data generated from different platforms can vary, and downstream analysis pipelines need to account for those platform-specific errors."
